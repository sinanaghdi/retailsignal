I’m sharing **RetailSignal**, a data analytics portfolio project built around the UCI Online Retail II dataset.

The goal was to answer practical retail questions with a reproducible workflow—not just build a dashboard. I worked through source schema inspection, data-quality auditing, transparent transaction rules, Python analysis, PostgreSQL modeling and window-function queries, repeat-purchase metrics, customer cohorts, RFM groupings, and a Power BI executive dashboard.

One important design choice was to keep recorded transaction value distinct from profit and to treat cancellation-prefixed invoices and negative quantities as evidence requiring careful interpretation, rather than automatically calling every record a completed return.
